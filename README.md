## Selected project and process
**Project:** Tax Management System for businesses in Rwanda.  
**Business process:** Business Tax Obligation Management and Compliance Monitoring.

The process allows a business to record tax obligations and payment deadlines, monitor paid and unpaid amounts, receive deadline reminders, estimate late-payment penalties for overdue balances, and generate tax compliance reports.

## Actors
- Business Owner / Taxpayer
- Tax Management System
- Notification Service
- Revenue Officer / Administrator

## Scope and assumptions
This is a business tracking and monitoring system, not an official Rwanda Revenue Authority platform. Payment entries are treated as user-recorded unless a payment integration is implemented. Penalty values are estimates based on rules configured in the application and should not be represented as official assessments. See the explanation document for the full assumptions.
